1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

CVBora AI is an AI-powered employability platform designed specifically for the Tanzanian job market. The platform enables students, graduates, and professionals to create professional, ATS-friendly, and HR-approved CVs using localized Tanzanian hiring standards.

The system guides users through educational history, skills, experience, certifications, and career goals, then intelligently generates optimized CVs based on:

- Industry standards
- Tanzanian recruitment expectations
- HR-approved formatting
- ATS compliance
- Career-specific optimization

The platform aims to bridge the gap between Tanzanian graduates and modern employment requirements while improving hiring quality for recruiters and employers.
